## 📊 Project Overview

The **E-Commerce Sales Performance & Customer Insights Dashboard** is a data analytics project developed using Microsoft Excel to analyze e-commerce transaction data and generate meaningful business insights. The project focuses on understanding sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product demand, order channels, geographical sales distribution, delivery performance, customer satisfaction, and weekly order trends. The purpose of this project is to transform raw transactional data into a structured analytical view that can help businesses understand their performance and make data-driven decisions.

## 🧮 KPI Summary

The main KPIs developed for this project provide an overall view of e-commerce business performance. The dataset contains **2,400 orders** and **11,997 units sold**, generating total sales of **$649,019.80**. The average customer rating is **3.96/5**, showing generally positive customer satisfaction, while the average delivery time is **2.34 days**, indicating relatively fast order fulfillment. These KPIs provide a high-level understanding of sales volume, revenue, customer experience, and operational performance.

## 🛍️ Product Analysis

Product analysis was performed to identify the products contributing the highest demand and revenue. T-Shirts recorded the highest quantity sold with **1,645 units**, followed by Jeans with **1,546 units** and Sneakers with **1,186 units**. From a sales-value perspective, Sneakers generated the highest sales amount at approximately **$127,637.50**, followed by Jeans at approximately **$112,893.20**. This comparison demonstrates the importance of analyzing both sales quantity and sales value because a product with high volume may not necessarily generate the highest revenue. These insights can help businesses improve inventory planning and promotional strategies.

## 📱 Order-Channel Analysis

The project analyzed different customer purchasing channels to identify where customers were placing their orders. The **App** was the leading channel with **868 orders**, **4,220 units**, and approximately **$229,393.50** in sales. The Website generated **569 orders** and approximately **$152,796.40** in sales, while Target.com generated **453 orders** and approximately **$118,568.80** in sales. Partner App generated **262 orders** with approximately **$75,134.90** in sales, and Instagram generated **248 orders** with approximately **$73,126.20** in sales. The results indicate that the App was the most important purchasing channel within the analyzed dataset.

## 👥 Customer Analysis

Customer analysis was conducted to understand purchasing behavior based on customer gender. Female customers accounted for the highest purchased quantity with **6,272 units**, followed by male customers with **4,672 units**. The dataset also contains transactions classified as Unknown and Other. This analysis provides an understanding of the customer composition represented in the dataset and can help businesses develop more targeted customer segmentation and marketing strategies.

## 📍 Location Analysis

The geographical analysis showed that the transactions in this dataset were from **California**. Among the counties analyzed, **Los Angeles County** was the strongest market, generating approximately **$215,551.20** in sales. San Diego County followed with approximately **$98,367.10**, while Sacramento County, Santa Clara County, and Alameda County also contributed significant sales. The results indicate that Los Angeles County was the dominant market and could be an important region for future marketing, customer acquisition, and sales strategies.

## 📦 Delivery Analysis

Delivery performance was analyzed using the Days to Deliver field. The average delivery time across the dataset was approximately **2.34 days**. A significant number of orders were delivered within one or two days, including **888 orders delivered in one day** and **538 orders delivered in two days**. There were also orders requiring three or more days for delivery. Overall, the results indicate relatively efficient delivery performance, while longer-delivery orders could be investigated to identify operational or logistics issues.

## ⭐ Rating Analysis

Customer satisfaction was evaluated using customer ratings. The overall average rating was **3.96 out of 5**. The dataset contained **721 orders with a 5-star rating**, **1,036 orders with a 4-star rating**, **495 orders with a 3-star rating**, **127 orders with a 2-star rating**, and **21 orders with a 1-star rating**. The majority of customers provided four- or five-star ratings, indicating generally positive customer satisfaction. Lower-rated orders can be investigated further to identify potential product, delivery, or service issues.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

The analysis provides several actionable recommendations for improving e-commerce performance. Since the App generated the highest order volume and sales, the business can prioritize the App as an important customer engagement and sales channel. Inventory planning should focus on high-demand products such as T-Shirts, Jeans, and Sneakers. High-revenue products such as Sneakers and Jeans can be supported with targeted promotional campaigns. Marketing efforts can also focus on high-performing geographical markets such as Los Angeles and San Diego. Orders receiving lower ratings should be investigated to understand possible product, delivery, or customer-service issues. Weekly order trends can be used for inventory planning and promotional campaigns, while orders taking more than three days to deliver can be analyzed to identify opportunities for improving logistics performance.
